# Introduction

Welcome to the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook.

This textbook covers four modules of Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics over a 13-week curriculum:

- **Module 1**: ROS2
- **Module 2**: Gazebo/Unity
- **Module 3**: NVIDIA Isaac
- **Module 4**: VLA (Vision-Language-Action Models)

Each module contains multiple chapters that progressively build your understanding of humanoid robotics and physical AI concepts.
